# Surface brief: Market buyer app (all routes)

Scope: the whole buyer app, web (≥1024) and app shell (<1024). Visitor mode: **Operate**. Audience, job and constraints live in PRODUCT.md and spec/; this brief keeps only the direction.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A marketplace that sells with the full Vietnamese rule set (three voucher slots, flash sale, COD, one order per shop) in a calm, image-led grammar, where every total can unfold into its lines. It refuses the category default: red banners, badge stacks on every card, countdown sirens, lump "Giảm giá".

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper ground with white working surfaces, one plum-ink text colour, jade as the single accent (brand mark, search, deal price, selection, focus). Literata serif for headings and product titles on the product page; Be Vietnam Pro for everything else. Borderless image-led cards at 4:5 with 8px corners; pills for every button and chip; black pill for the primary action; hairline rules, no card borders, soft warm shadows only on overlays.

STORY: The buyer browses and decides by looking at products, not promotions; deals are present in one quiet voice; at cart, checkout, order and return she can open any total and see each đồng: goods, each voucher by name, shipping, the split per shop.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Web Home: header (logo, Danh mục, full-width search pill with jade button, Thông báo, Yêu thích, Giỏ hàng) over a slim editorial row; then a serif headline with a 3×2 image mosaic and one black pill, followed by round category tiles. App Home: logo + search + cart, mosaic, tiles, tab bar.

FORM: Pinned by the owner, no roll. seed: none (pinned). "Visual: Grammar, không lấy brand" and "Thay ref sang Esty đi", the grammar measured in vault note 10-projects/market/etsy-style-study.md. Signature move: **the money fold**, every total (cart bar, checkout, order, return refund) is a disclosure that unfolds into a ledger of named lines, with a thin proportional bar showing how a session discount splits across shops.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
